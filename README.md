# Motiflo — online melody maker / 在线旋律创作工具

![Motiflo logo](assets/logo.png)

[Try Motiflo](https://motiflo.win/) · [Listen to 12 examples](https://motiflo.win/listen/) · [Free plan](https://motiflo.win/pricing/) · [How to use it](https://motiflo.win/help/) · [FAQ](FAQ.md)

Motiflo turns a small musical idea into melody sketches you can hear, read, save, and export. It is a browser-based melody maker with piano, violin, voice, acoustic guitar, electric guitar, band, and orchestra options. Enter your own opening notes or choose a style, mood, key, harmony, meter, and tempo, then compare up to three candidates in one batch. Motiflo uses an algorithmic composition engine; it is not a generative audio model.

## What you can do now

- Shape a melody with instrument, style, mood, key, chords, meter, tempo, and phrase length. You can also provide up to eight opening notes.
- Play each candidate and view its sheet music. Print the score or save it as a PDF in your browser.
- Develop a favorite sketch into a full song.
- Export the selected melody as MIDI, MusicXML, WAV, or a Motiflo project JSON file.
- Save works to your private account library, rename them, move them to Trash, and restore them. Unsynced drafts remain in your browser until you choose to sync.
- Explore [12 public listening examples](https://motiflo.win/listen/) before creating an account.

For a closer look at the workflow, see [how to make a melody from your own notes](https://motiflo.win/guides/melody-from-notes/) and [how to export MIDI and MusicXML](https://motiflo.win/guides/export-midi-musicxml/).
