# Motiflo FAQ / 常见问题

## What can I make?

Short melody sketches for piano, violin, or voice. You can guide them with your own notes, style, mood, harmony, and other musical settings. [Try the workbench](https://motiflo.win/app/).

## What can I export?

Sheet music can be printed or saved as PDF in the browser. You can also export MIDI, MusicXML, WAV, and a project file. [Read the help page](https://motiflo.win/help/).

## How does the free allowance work?

An account can complete three generation batches per UTC week. The allowance resets Monday at 00:00 UTC. A batch can contain up to three candidate melodies. Failed generations do not consume a completed batch.

## What does Lifetime Pro include?

Lifetime Pro removes the weekly generation quota for the purchasing Motiflo account while the service operates. Rate, concurrency, and fair-use limits remain. It does not include unlimited server capacity or separate future products. [See the current terms](https://motiflo.win/legal/).

## Is Motiflo an AI audio generator?

No. Motiflo creates symbolic melody sketches with an algorithmic composition engine and renders them for listening and export.

## How do I hear examples before registering?

[Listen to twelve samples](https://motiflo.win/listen/) without creating an account.

## 免费次数什么时候刷新？

每周一 UTC 00:00 刷新，对应北京时间周一 08:00。免费账号每周可成功生成三组，每组最多三首候选旋律。

## “永久会员”包含什么？

在 Motiflo 服务运营期间，购买账号不受每周生成次数限制；请求速率、并发和合理使用限制仍然适用。详情请查看[官网说明](https://motiflo.win/legal/)。
