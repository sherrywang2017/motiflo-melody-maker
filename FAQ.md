# Motiflo FAQ / 常见问题

## What can I make?

Short melody sketches with piano, violin, voice, acoustic guitar, electric guitar, band, or orchestra. Choose musical settings or enter up to eight opening notes, then compare up to three candidates. You can develop a favorite sketch into a full song. [Try the workbench](https://motiflo.win/app/).

## What can I export?

You can export MIDI, MusicXML, WAV, and a Motiflo project JSON file. Print the sheet music or save it as a PDF through your browser. [Read the help page](https://motiflo.win/help/).

## How does the free allowance work?

A free account can complete three generation batches per UTC week. The allowance resets Monday at 00:00 UTC (08:00 in Beijing). Each batch can contain up to three candidate melodies. Failed or empty generations do not consume a completed batch.

## Where are my saved works?

Saved projects are private to your verified account. Open the workbench Library to load or rename them, move them to Trash, or restore them. A failed sync keeps an account-specific draft in your browser until you choose to sync. [Open the workbench](https://motiflo.win/app/).

## Is Motiflo an AI audio generator?

No. Motiflo creates symbolic melody sketches with an algorithmic composition engine and renders them for listening and export.

## Can I listen before registering?

Yes. [Listen to twelve examples](https://motiflo.win/listen/) without an account.

## Can I view, correct, export, or delete my account data?

Yes. You can view your account and saved projects after signing in, export individual works, and manage works in the Library. For an account data copy, correction, or deletion request, follow the instructions in the [privacy policy](https://motiflo.win/legal/) or write to [pawdoodleofficial@gmail.com](mailto:pawdoodleofficial@gmail.com).

## 免费次数什么时候刷新？

每周一 UTC 00:00，也就是北京时间周一 08:00。免费账号每周可成功生成三组，每组最多三首候选旋律；失败或空结果不计入已完成次数。

## 我的作品存在哪里？

已保存作品属于你的私人账号，保存在 Motiflo 自有服务器上；未同步的草稿留在当前浏览器。账号数据的查询、导出、更正和删除方式见[隐私政策](https://motiflo.win/legal/)。
